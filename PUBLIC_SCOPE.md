# Public CI boundary

Only original or license-cleared source and synthetic fixtures may be committed. No private media, databases, secrets, third-party SDK binaries or unapproved code. Production data remains read-only. Use only free GitHub-hosted standard runners. Keep legacy product builds and safety gates intact. WinUI 3 remains an isolated experiment until parity and comparable Windows performance are demonstrated. Zero-step jobs indicate runner, billing or policy investigation, not a proven compiler failure.

Coordination remains in private lxsdd/dev-infrastructure issue #18 and draft PR #19; do not copy their private contents here.
